Control

One command byte per button

START (0xAA) runs the mission (line at 100 mm/s, barcodes and junction moves live), STOP (0xA9) aborts it and brakes. After STOP or a lift the car goes back to IDLE by itself; a fault (IMU, collision, ...) stays until Reset (0xAB) clears it with the car standing still. The bench image's Reset zeroes the wheel distance.
