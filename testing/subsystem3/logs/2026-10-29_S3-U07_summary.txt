What the sensor sees at 2 cm: the six marks are the six thick (18 mm) bars.
The nine thin (6 mm) bars move the reading by about +30-40 over white (noise
+-25): they are not resolvable. The sensor reacts strongly only to dark areas
about 18 mm wide (the track line reads ~1975, an 18 mm bar ~2000-2200), so the
spec-size card (3/9 mm bars) reads as white, as the parked check showed.
The car samples the sensor every ~0.56 mm (1450 samples over 806 mm), so the
sampling rate is not the limit.
